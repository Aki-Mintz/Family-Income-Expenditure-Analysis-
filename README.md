# Household Income & Expenditure Analysis in the Philippines

## Project Overview

This SQL project analyzes household income, expenditure patterns, and demographic characteristics of Filipino families. The objective is to identify income distribution across regions, understand spending behavior, and examine how household characteristics influence financial outcomes.

The project demonstrates SQL skills in data exploration, data cleaning, aggregation, filtering, subqueries, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), window functions, views, and analytical reporting.

## Objectives

- Identify regions with higher and lower household income levels.
- Analyze spending behavior across expenditure categories.
- Examine relationships between income and household demographics.
- Compare household income and expenditure patterns.
- Generate insights that can support socio-economic analysis and decision-making.

## Key Insights

### 1. National Capital Region (NCR) Shows Strong Financial Performance

Analysis of high-income households indicates that NCR contains the largest concentration of households earning above ₱90,000. This suggests stronger earning opportunities and greater economic activity compared to many other regions.

### 2. CALABARZON and Central Luzon Also Contain Many High-Income Households

Regions such as CALABARZON and Central Luzon consistently appear among regions with higher-income households, highlighting their importance as major economic centers outside NCR.

### 3. Household Spending Increases Alongside Income

Higher-income households generally exhibit larger expenditures across food, utilities, transportation, leisure, and education categories. Increased income appears to support greater consumption and investment in household needs.

### 4. Education Spending Varies Across Regions

Average education expenditures differ significantly between regions, indicating disparities in educational investment and access among Filipino households.

### 5. Food Remains a Major Household Expense

Staple food, meat, and seafood expenditures account for a substantial portion of total household spending, emphasizing the importance of food-related expenses in family budgets.

### 6. Female-Led Households Represent a Meaningful Share of Families

Regional analysis shows that female-headed households constitute a significant percentage of total households, demonstrating the growing role of women in household financial management.

### 7. Income Distribution Is Uneven Across Regions

The analysis reveals noticeable differences in average household income across regions, reflecting varying levels of economic development and employment opportunities.

### 8. Savings Potential Varies Considerably

Estimated savings calculations indicate that some households maintain substantial income after expenses, while others allocate a large share of earnings toward essential expenditures.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw household survey data into meaningful insights. Through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, aggregations, window functions, and analytical reporting, the project identifies regional income differences, spending behaviors, and demographic patterns among Filipino households.

The findings highlight income disparities across regions, the importance of food and education expenditures, and the concentration of high-income households in economically developed areas such as NCR, CALABARZON, and Central Luzon.
